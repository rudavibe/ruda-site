Ruda

A private assistant for one person. Not a public service.

Ruda is a personal AI assistant run by its owner on a private server. It helps the owner organise notes, draft documents and keep schedules. With the owner's permission, it reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets.

Ruda has no other users, does not accept sign-ups and does not offer its Google connection to anyone else.
